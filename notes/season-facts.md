# Survivor 51 — season facts

Gathered Sep 28, 2026 (after Episode 1, before Episode 2).

## Season basics
- Premiere: Wednesday, Sep 23, 2026, CBS. Mamanuca Islands, Fiji. 26-day game, 21 castaways (11 women, 10 men).
- Branded "The Open Era": any past twist, idol or advantage can appear at any time.
- Million-dollar coin toss: a player may call a coin flip. Right call adds $1M to the prize; wrong call means immediate elimination and the $1M rolls to next season. Not used by anyone in Episode 1 as far as recaps show.

## Episode 1 — "Permanent Uncertainty" (Sep 23, 2026)
- **Exile Island:** Lewis volunteered, went to Exile with no supplies, and was safe from the first vote. He tried to chop down a mast with a small axe to win an idol and ran out of time. **No idol for Lewis.**
- **Joining a tribe:** Lewis joined the tribe that lost immunity: **Toka**.
- **Supply challenge:** Kristin (Savu) and Kilby (Toka) were picked for their tribes and agreed to split the supplies (per Wikipedia). No reward challenge otherwise.
- **Immunity:** Savu won. Toka lost.
- **Idols:** Rob (Savu) found the Savu camp hidden immunity idol. Plain idol, no twist attached; reportedly good through the final five. **Rob still holds it.**
- **Shot in the Dark:** Aaliyah and Jenna both played it. Both "not safe." Neither voted as a result.
- **Vote (Toka):** Aaliyah out 6-2.
  - For Aaliyah: Brady, Kilby, Maggie, Mike, Patt, Thien An.
  - For Jenna: Devin, Jelly.
- **After Ep 1:** Savu 10, Toka 10 (Lewis in, Aaliyah out).
- Confessionals: Devin most (12), Cristian fewest (1). Source: Inside Survivor stats.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- The Paramount+ recap page (as summarized) gave the vote as 5-3. Wikipedia, TVLine, Inside Survivor and the fandom wiki all say 6-2, and Inside Survivor lists who voted for whom. Using 6-2.
- Inside Survivor titles its recap "The Open Era" and dates it Sep 24. That is their headline and publish date, not the episode title.
- Cristian's age: CBS says 26; the fandom wiki says 25. Using 26.
- Hometowns in `cast.json` use the current residence (what the show chyron and most sites list). CBS gives separate hometowns for many, e.g. Aaliyah (Gloucester City, NJ), Lewis (Dublin, Ireland), Brady (La Salle, IL).
- Thien An's CBS full name is written An "Thien An" Nguyen. `cast.json` uses "Thien An Nguyen".
- Whether the Toka camp idol has been found: nothing reported yet.
- Rob's idol "good through final five" comes from Inside Survivor's recap; not checked elsewhere.
