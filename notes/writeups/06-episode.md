A quiet night on the scoreboard, folks. Only three castaways scored. But the pack behind the leader just got very, very crowded.

## On the island

Each tribe sent one person on a boat ride: **Kristin** from Toka, **Ori** from Savu and **Sharonda** from Dina. There were two plates of ribs and corn for three people. Kristin volunteered to go hungry and stay on Exile, and walked away with a **tethered idol**. The fine print: Kristin can only play it to save Ori or Sharonda. If both of them go home, it becomes a real idol. Hold that thought.

Toka edged Dina by a hair in the immunity challenge, and Savu lost the puzzle. That sent the last three purple castaways to Tribal. Ori worked hard to split **Devin** and **Jelly**. Jelly answered by showing everyone her idol. Ori played his Shot in the Dark, and it came back "Not Safe." **Ori** went home, 2–0. Which means Kristin's idol is now tethered to one person.
