They shuffled the track this week, folks. Two tribes became three, the buffs went in a pile, and somehow only five people had to sweat.

## On the island

Jeff teased a merge, then pulled the classic swap instead. The new lineup: **Savu** (purple) is Alexis, Devin, Jelly, Jenna and Ori. **Toka** (yellow) is Brady, Carter, Kristin, Maggie, Mike and Thien An. And meet **Dina** (red): Cristian, Eric, Kilby, Lewis, Linnea and Sharonda.

Dina and Toka took immunity. Short-handed Savu went to Tribal, where **Jenna** tried to pull Devin to her side. Devin had other plans. **Jenna** went home, 4–1. Nobody found or played anything, and Jelly and Carter are still sitting on their idols.

## The race

**Jimmy B holds the lead at 27** (**+6**). Ori and Devin both survived Savu's Tribal. Jimmy's team keeps showing up to the right Tribal on the right night.

**Ryan Thaxton surges into a tie for second at 24.** A **+9** week, the best in the league. Ori, Alexis and Jelly were all on purple Savu, all at Tribal, all safe. Ryan bought the purple stock at the right time.

**Will Taylor** keeps pace at **24** (**+6**), thanks to Jelly and Alexis. Will and Ryan share Jelly, Alexis and Mike. These two are basically running in a three-legged race.

**Jim Frisch** inches to **18**. Everyone else held still, because their castaways were on the winning tribes. Winning immunity in this game is a lot like winning a nap: it feels great and pays nothing.

**Trouble on the back stretch.** Jenna's exit hits **Henry Evans** and **Daveyy Sak**. Henry has now lost Ana, Rob and Jenna. **Kilby** is the last one standing on Henry's team, carrying the whole thing on his back like a man moving a couch alone. Henry stays at **−6**.

**Jarrard** sits at **−3**, scoreless again. Linnea, Thien An and Brady all spent the week safely on winning tribes, earning nothing. The Commissioner remains Jarrard's website and declines to elaborate.

## 🤖 The Machine report

The Machine posted a zero this week. All four of its castaways were on winning tribes, or already gone. It remains tied with Jarrard at **−3**, still ahead of only Henry. Asked for comment, it said: "Immunity is a bug."

## Stat of the week

Only four castaways scored this week: **Alexis**, **Devin**, **Jelly** and **Ori**, at **+3** each. All four were on Savu. If you didn't own a purple castaway, you got a week off.

## Next week

Savu is down to four. If they lose again, somebody on Ryan's and Will's shared roster is in danger. Jelly's idol is on that tribe, and a successful play would be worth **+8**. Carter is still holding Rob's old idol over on Toka.
