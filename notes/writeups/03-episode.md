Folks, we have a driver leaving the race on foot. In twenty years of calling these things, I have never seen a man exit the track to go check on a cut.

## On the island

**Rob** saw the medic about his leg. The medic found a minor cut. Rob quit anyway, and handed his idol to **Carter** on the way out. Carter, meanwhile, went on a journey to Toka, missed the boat home, and is now a member of Toka. Nobody asked Carter. **Devin** and **Maggie** each picked up an extra vote.

**Savu** won immunity, so **Toka** went to Tribal with eleven people, Carter included. **Patt** went home, 6–5 over **Kilby**.

## The race

**Jimmy B takes the lead!** A **+15 pts** week vaults Jimmy from the pack to first place at **21**. Carter survived and inherited an idol (**+6**), Devin found an extra vote (**+6**), and Lewis held on (**+3**). Jimmy drafted the right people *and* the right accidents.

**Will Taylor** surges into second at **18** (**+12**). Maggie's extra vote did a lot of heavy lifting.

**Daveyy Sak**, last week's clean zero, posts **+12** and climbs four spots to a tie for sixth. A comeback story already.

**Jim**, **Martin** and **Ryan** share third at **15**. Jim and Ryan were last week's co-leaders. Both are now looking at Jimmy's taillights.

**The penalty box.** Quitting costs **−15 pts**. That's the same rule we used last season; nobody quit then, so nobody noticed. Rob's walk-off hits three teams:

- **Jarrard** drops to **−3**, and also picked Rob to *win the whole thing*. The Commissioner has no comment. The Commissioner is Jarrard's website.
- **Henry Evans** falls to **−6** and last place, after losing Ana *and* Rob in two weeks. That's half a team gone before the first merge rumors.

## 🤖 The Machine report

The Machine drafted Rob first overall and described him as "a man with a contingency plan." The contingency was leaving. The Machine is at **−3**, tied with Jarrard and ahead of only Henry. It has issued a statement: "Recalibrating."

## Stat of the week

Four people picked **Patt** to win the season: **Ryan**, **Will**, **Martin** and **Matt-I'm a survivor I'm not gon' give up**. Patt was voted out in Episode 3. Those winner bets are now decorative.

## Next week

Carter is holding Rob's idol on his brand-new tribe. That's worth **+8 pts** to Jimmy and Martin if Carter plays it to save someone, and **−5** if he goes home with it. Savu is down to seven. If they keep winning, they'll keep getting zero points for it. Strange sport.
