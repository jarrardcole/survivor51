Two Tribal Councils in one night, folks. Two torches out, two extra votes cast, and one league leader who suddenly can't see anyone in the rear-view mirror.

## On the island

Toka won immunity and a pile of pastries, which left Savu and Dina to vote. Before the challenge dust settled, **Maggie** was randomly sent from Toka on a visit to Dina. She picked up a brand-new extra vote there, then handed one extra vote each to **Kilby** and **Lewis**, on one condition: vote out Linnea. Maggie went home to Toka with no advantage and a lot of influence.

Over at Savu, **Devin** flashed an extra vote at **Alexis** and asked her to write down Ori. Alexis didn't buy it and pitched Devin instead. Jelly stuck with the Helicopter Alliance. **Alexis** went home, 3–1. Then Dina got weird. Kilby and Lewis played their extra votes, Linnea and Lewis tied 4–4, the revote deadlocked, and the other four had to agree or draw rocks. Nobody wanted rocks. **Linnea** went home by consensus. Lewis's "Blood Ring" turned out to be a bluff, which is the most Survivor sentence ever written.

## The race

**Jimmy B runs away with it at 43** (**+16**, best in the league). Lewis cashed in an extra vote and survived (**+10**), and Devin and Ori both made it through Savu (**+3** each). Jimmy now leads by 13. Everyone else is racing for second.

**Ryan Thaxton** and **Will Taylor** stay tied for second at **30** (**+6** each). Jelly survived again for both. Ryan got Ori; Will got Maggie's journey (**+3**). Still a three-legged race. Still going fine.

**Jim Frisch** holds fourth at **27** (**+9**). Devin, Sharonda and Eric each survived for **+3**.

**The big movers:** **Daveyy Sak** and **Matt Jennings** both jump to a tie for fifth at **25** (**+13**). Daveyy rode Kilby (**+10**) and Maggie (**+3**). Matt rode Lewis (**+10**) and Eric (**+3**), even while losing Linnea.

**Martin** slips to seventh at **18** (**+3**, Cristian). **Matt-I'm a survivor I'm not gon' give up** is eighth at **15** (**+6**), on Sharonda and Cristian.

**Henry Evans finally gets off the floor.** Kilby, the last one standing on Henry's team, posted a **+10** and lifted Henry to **4**. One castaway, carrying the whole roster up a staircase.

**Trouble:** **Jarrard** drops to last at **−3**. Linnea went home, and Thien An and Brady napped on immune Toka. The Commissioner is Jarrard's website and has been advised to say nothing further.

## 🤖 The Machine report

The Machine owned Kilby. The Machine is now at **7**, ahead of Henry and Jarrard. It has issued a short statement: "Two humans gave a third human extra votes. I appreciate the efficiency." It then asked whether Linnea's spot on Dina is available.

## Stat of the week

**Kilby** and **Lewis** each scored **+10**: survive, receive an advantage, play it with effect. That's more than any whole roster scored last week. Maggie gave away the votes and still got **+3** for it.

## Next week

Savu is down to three: Devin, Jelly and Ori. Another loss means somebody on Jimmy's, Ryan's or Will's team goes home. Jelly still has an idol (**+8** if played well), Devin still has an extra vote, and Carter is still holding Rob's old idol on Toka.
