Folks, it's merge night, and the payout window is open. Everyone still standing collected a bonus, and the scoreboard looks like a slot machine just hit.

## On the island

The tribes merged into **Tovuto** (teal), thirteen strong. **Mike** won the first individual immunity of the season. **Brady**'s plan leaked before Tribal, his redirect onto Eric didn't land, and Brady went home as the first member of the jury. Over in the idol department, **Kristin**'s tethered idol had everybody whispering.

## The race

Merge nights are generous: **+4** for every castaway who made it, **+3** for surviving Tribal, and **+5** for immunity. That adds up fast.

**Jimmy B still leads at 67** (**+21**). Carter, Lewis and Devin all made the merge and survived. It's a quiet, steady lead.

**But here comes Jim Frisch!** The best week in the league so far: **+28**, up to **61** and second place. Jim is the only player with all four castaways still alive: Kristin, Devin, Sharonda and Eric. Jim drafted a team that simply refuses to leave.

**Will Taylor** surges to **59** (**+26**). Mike's immunity win did the heavy lifting: **+12** from Mike alone.

**Ryan Thaxton** (52) and **Matt Jennings** (49) round out the top five. **Daveyy Sak** and **Martin** share sixth at 39.

**The back of the pack is finally moving.** **Jarrard** climbs out of negative territory for the first time since Rob walked off, all the way to **8**. Thien An and Brady both collected merge money before Brady's torch went out. **Henry Evans** gets **+7** from Kilby, still a team of one, and still going.

## 🤖 The Machine report

The Machine had a very good night: **+26**, up to **36**. Mike's immunity, Kilby and Kristin all paid out. It is now ahead of three humans, and it would like those three to know it is not gloating. It is gloating.

## Stat of the week

**186 points** went to the league this week. The previous best week was 82. Merge night is Christmas.
